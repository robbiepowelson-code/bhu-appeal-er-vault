6 3: I submit this declaration in response to the Declaration of Peter Radu filed March 17,

7 2026, which contains numerous inaccuracies, omissions, and misleading statements

9 stale shelter offers—many from 2022, 2023 and 2024—that do not reflect current 10 circumstances or the City’s ongoing obligations under the ADA. 11 4. This is false as no recent offers of shelter have been 17 made despite BHU and its members requesting shelter and housing options. 18 6. Even Radu’s declaration contradicts what he said to me on email. On February 18, 2026, 19 I sent an email to Peter Radu, Okeya Vance-Dozier, and Epsalon Galloway formally 20 requesting interim housing for Erik Alexander Johnson Coon, a disabled BHU member. 21 Attached as Exhibit A is a true and correct copy of this email. Radu responded on 22 February 19, 2026, stating: “Your note and request have been received and we will 23 discuss this directly with this person, as is our normal practice, the next time we 24 encounter them during outreach to the encampment.” 25 7. To date, no HRT staff member has discussed shelter with Mr. Coon after I made this 26 request for him. Despite Radu’s promise to “discuss this directly with this person” during 27 outreach, HRT staff have visited the encampment numerous times without ever engaging 28 Mr. Coon. His tent is visible. He is present, but they simply walk past.
