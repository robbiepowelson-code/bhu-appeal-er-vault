Broadening a putative class of Plaintiffs would exacerbate the harm to the public interest caused by the injunction that is already in place. The Court should also consider the interests of the community immediately surrounding the encampment. The City has provided declarations from neighboring residents and business testify to conditions that conditions include, garbage and other debris obstructing sidewalks and roadways, fires, open drug use and discarded drug paraphernalia, uncontrolled dogs, and threats of physical violence from individuals living in the encampment. The Court should consider the interests of these residents and business owners before expanding the injunction currently in place and allowing unsafe, unsanitary, and dangerous conditions at the encampment to fester even longer than they already have. The public interest supports denying any request to expand the current injunction. Finally, broadening the existing injunction in the manner Plaintiffs request would effectively deny the City the opportunity to respond to Plaintiffs class certification motion.
