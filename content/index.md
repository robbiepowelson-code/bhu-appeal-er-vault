# BHU Appeal ER Vault — Start Here

Everything from the **Berkeleys Opening Brief** folder, converted into a searchable Obsidian vault.

**The case:** Ninth Circuit Nos. 26-2755 & 26-2823 — *Berkeley Homeless Union, et al. v. City of Berkeley* (N.D. Cal. 3:25-cv-01414-EMC), consolidated with *Prado, et al. v. City of Berkeley* (3:23-cv-04537-EMC). The City (Appellant) filed its opening brief and a 12-volume, 2,197-page Excerpts of Record. This vault exists to help you find record evidence for your **opposition (answering) brief**.

## What's in here

- **Documents/** — one note per ER document (128 total), sorted into Orders & Rulings, Transcripts, Declarations, Exhibits, Motions & Briefs, and Docket Sheets. Each note has the full text with an `## ER-###` heading for every record page, so a search hit tells you the exact ER page to cite.
- **Appeal Filings/** — the City's Opening Brief, its Addendum, and the ER Index Volume as filed.
- **Evidence Workspace/** — working notes: an [[Evidence Log]] to collect quotes with cites, and [[Opposition Brief Points]] to map each argument to its supporting record evidence.
- **Source PDFs/** — the original PDFs, so you can open the actual filed pages.

## How to find evidence (see [[02 How to Query This Vault]])

1. Open search (Cmd-Shift-F) and search a phrase, name, or date.
2. The hit shows which document — and which `## ER-###` section — contains it.
3. Record the cite in **[[Evidence Log]]** using the `volume-ER-page` format shown at the top of every note (e.g. `3-ER-402`).

Pages that were image-only scans were OCR'd; their text appears under "[OCR of scanned page:]" and may contain OCR artifacts — verify wording against the Source PDF before quoting in the brief.
