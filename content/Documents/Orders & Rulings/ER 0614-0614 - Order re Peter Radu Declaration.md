DECLARATION
[[ER 0615-0618 - Declaration of Peter Radu re Inspection of Vehicles Pursuant to Court’…|Docket No. 193]]
The Court is in receipt of the Declaration of Peter Redu regarding the inspection of 
vehicles around the Harrison Corridor. Radu Declaration ([[ER 0615-0618 - Declaration of Peter Radu re Inspection of Vehicles Pursuant to Court’…|Dkt. 193]]). The Radu Declaration 
presents particularized evidence of threats to public safety from certain vehicles in the area. 
Plaintiffs are directed to respond to the Radu Declaration no later than Friday, March 13, 
2026, at 12:00 p.m. 
The Court’s March 7, 2026 order prohibiting the City from seizing, impounding, or 
destroying vehicles in the abatement zone remains in full effect. TRO Order ([[ER 0833-0837 - Order Granting in Part and Denying in Part Motion for TRO|Dkt. 187]]). The City 
is directed not to move, seize, impound, or destroy any vehicles covered by the Court’s order until 
further order by the Court. 
IT IS SO ORDERED.
