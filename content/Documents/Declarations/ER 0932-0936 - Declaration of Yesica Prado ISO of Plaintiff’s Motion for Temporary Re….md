DECLARATION OF YESICA PRADO
I, Yesica Prado, declare as follows:
1. I am a Plaintiff in the above-entitled action. I am over the age of eighteen, and I have 
personal knowledge of the facts in this declaration. If called as a witness, I could and would 
competently testify to the matters stated herein. I make this declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion 
for Temporary Restraining Order. 
2. I live in my vehicle, which is currently parked on 8th Street between Harrison and Gilman 
Streets. I have lived in this area since about 2018. In the nearly six years that I have lived here, I have 
seen many encampment residents come and go, but I work hard to get to know my neighbors and have 
known some residents for years. 
3. In the last year, I have witnessed a large increase in the population of people at 8th and 
Harrison. Many new people have reported to me that they are coming here after being swept from areas 
around City Hall in Downtown Berkeley, Ohlone Park, Allston Way and the Ohlone Greenway. Some 
unhoused people who work during the day or stay elsewhere closer to services they need in the day also 
come to this area just to sleep at night because they want to be close to community. At this point, I 
believe there to be approximately 35 to 40 people who seek shelter at 8th and Harrison, but there may be 
more who pass through and receive temporary support. 
4. I am a qualified person with a disability. I have been diagnosed with Post Traumatic 
Stress Disorder (PTSD) and Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder (ADHD). As a result of these 
disabilities, I need stability and the safety of community to function in my daily life. A large percentage 
of my neighbors also have disclosed to me that they have disabilities. I have helped many of them seek 
disability accommodations to various City actions. 
5. I have seen the Notice of Encampment Closure posted by the City of Berkeley on 
February 17, 2026, February 18, 2026, and February 19, 2026. 
6. On March 2, 2026, the City re-noticed the encampment closure with a compliance 
deadline of March 9, 2026. This notice was posted throughout the Harrison encampment, including on 
fences, the encampment banner, and other locations scattered throughout the encampment. I observed 
that the notice was posted directly on my RV. However, it was not placed on the surrounding dwellings.
