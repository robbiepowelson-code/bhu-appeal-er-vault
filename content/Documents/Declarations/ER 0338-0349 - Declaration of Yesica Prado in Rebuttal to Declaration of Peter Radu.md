3 1. I have personal knowledge of the facts set forth in this declaration.

4 2. 1 am a witness to this case. Iam member of the Berkeley Homeless Union (BHU) and a

5 resident of the encampment at 8" and Harrison Streets.

6 3: I submit this declaration in response to the Declaration of Peter Radu filed March 17,

7 2026, which contains numerous inaccuracies, omissions, and misleading statements

8 regarding shelter offers made to BHU members. His declaration relies almost entirely on

9 stale shelter offers—many from 2022, 2023 and 2024—that do not reflect current
10 circumstances or the City's ongoing obligations under the ADA.
11 4. Mr. Radu's declaration lists only 22 individuals. But this Court's Temporary Restraining
12 Order covers 28 individuals. Radu fails to mention six people entirely, meaning the City
13 has no record of offering them any shelter at all.
14 Lack of Context in Radu's Declaration
15 5. Mr. Radu's declaration creates the false impression that the City has repeatedly offered
16 accessible shelter to BHU members. This is false as no recent offers of shelter have been
17 made despite BHU and its members requesting shelter and housing options.
18 6. Even Radu’s declaration contradicts what he said to me on email. On February 18, 2026,
19 I sent an email to Peter Radu, Okeya Vance-Dozier, and Epsalon Galloway formally
20 requesting interim housing for Erik Alexander Johnson Coon, a disabled BHU member.
21 Attached as Exhibit A is a true and correct copy of this email. Radu responded on
22 February 19, 2026, stating: "Your note and request have been received and we will
23 discuss this directly with this person, as is our normal practice, the next time we
24 encounter them during outreach to the encampment."
25 7. To date, no HRT staff member has discussed shelter with Mr. Coon after I made this
26 request for him. Despite Radu's promise to "discuss this directly with this person" during
27 outreach, HRT staff have visited the encampment numerous times without ever engaging
28 Mr. Coon. His tent is visible. He is present, but they simply walk past.
