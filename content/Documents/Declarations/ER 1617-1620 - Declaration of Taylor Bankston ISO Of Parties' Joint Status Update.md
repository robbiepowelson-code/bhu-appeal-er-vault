I, Taylar Bankston, declare as follows: 
1. I have personal knowledge of the facts set forth in this declaration and, if called as
a witness, could and would testify competently to the matters set forth below. 
2. I have been a resident of University Village in Berkeley, California since
September 2023. University Village is located adjacent to the Harrison Corridor encampment, 
and I regularly observe conditions in and around the encampment while coming to and from my 
residence. 
3. Since moving into University Village, conditions associated with the encampment
have affected my daily life, my safety, and my ability to use and enjoy my home.

7. Over the past year, conditions associated with the encampment have worsened. I 
have observed that the number of people and the physical footprint of the encampment has 
increased, with more debris, more animals, and more frequent incidents spilling into University 
Village. 
8. I have also observed individuals throwing objects, screaming at passersby, and 
acting aggressively in public spaces. 
9. I have changed my daily routines and schedule due to safety concerns. Because it 
gets dark earlier during parts of the year, I changed my work schedule so that I can walk my dogs 
around 4:00 p.m. while there is still daylight. I avoid walking outside at night, avoid certain 
routes entirely, and only use well-lit paths. I carry pepper spray with me often, and I limit when I 
check my mail or take out trash to daylight hours. These are not preferences; they are safety 
measures I feel are necessary to keep myself safe. 
10. I have witnessed repeated criminal activity that I have witnessed coming from the 
encampment. I have seen video footage taken by a neighbor of this man that I recognize from the 
encampment follow Amazon delivery drivers with a black trash bag picking up packages and 
stealing them immediately after they are delivered. Neighbors routinely collect packages for me, 
and for one another to prevent theft. I have personally had multiple packages stolen and have 
video footage of three thefts. I have found opened packages belonging to neighbors discarded 
near the encampment area, particularly on the side of University Village closest to the 
encampment. 
11. I have also observed what appears to be an ongoing bicycle theft operation. There 
is an individual I have personally witnessed stealing bicycles and bike parts. I recognize this 
individual as someone who resides in the encampment, and neighbors routinely confront him to 
recover stolen property.

produce fumes and smoke that drift toward University Village. In September, 2024, a significant 
fire occurred in the encampment. I heard a loud explosion and saw a large plume of smoke near

14. Because University Village is subsidized housing for students, I can afford to
reside here, but I cannot afford to live elsewhere. Due to how the conditions of the encampment 
have impacted me, I am part of a safety committee within the Village, and I have personally spent 
approximately over 60 hours just in the past six months alone organizing safety meetings, 
responding to incidents, and addressing encampment-related concerns amongst University 
Village residents. These responsibilities detract from my academic research and studies as a 
graduate student. 
15. The cumulative effect of these conditions has left me feeling constantly unsafe and
anxious in my own home. My family and partner are deeply concerned for my safety. Although 
many residents have become resigned to these conditions, we should not have to accept living in 
fear in student housing.
