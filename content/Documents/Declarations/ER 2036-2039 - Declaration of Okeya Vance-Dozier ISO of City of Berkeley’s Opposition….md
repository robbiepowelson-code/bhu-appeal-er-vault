2. I am a City of Berkeley ("City") Community Services Specialist II working under 
the supervision of Assistant to the City Manager Peter Radu. I am directly involved in work of the 
City's Homeless Response Team ("HRT"). The HRT is a multi-departmental group of City staff 
working together to address the needs of the unhoused population in Berkeley. My duties include 
direct outreach to unhoused individuals and coordinating the activities of other members of the 
HR T. Any documents or correspondence referred to in this declaration are kept by the City in the 
regular course of business, and it is a regular practice to make and retain such records. 
3. On January 7, 2025, I, along with another member of the HRT and Berkeley 
Police, posted 50 copies of a Public Notice throughout the encampment area on Harrison Street 
from 10th Street to 6th Street and on 8th Street from Gilman Street to Codomices Creek (the 
"Harrison Corridor"). A true and correct coy of the January 7, 2025 Public Notice is attached 
hereto as Exhibit A. 
4. It was observed that the Fact Sheet attached to the January 7, 2025 Public Notice 
included typos incorrectly stating that the Public Notice had been distributed on January 7, 2024, 
and that the City Manager had declared a public nuisance on December 18, 2025. Therefore, on 
January 10, 2025 I, along with another member ofHRT posted 50 corrected Fact Sheets next to 
the Public Notices already in place. A true and correct copy of the corrected Fact Sheet posted on 
January 10, 2025 is attached hereto as Exhibit B.

encampment residents' appeal of the City Manager's nuisance declaration and order to abate the

12. HRT holds a group call referred to as a case conference every non-holiday 
Monday when community partners, including HAC, EBCLC, Lifelong, and others, are invited to 
bring any individual needing services up for discussion. The City's shelter operators are on the 
call as well to inform the group what options may be available, including non-congregate and 
congregate shelters. These community groups work with individuals residing in and around 
Harrison Street, and knew about the abatement notices. Through these weekly calls, if any of the 
Harrison Corridor residents moved to another location, HRT would be able to work with these 
community partners, including Lifelong, to enable the continuity of their services.
