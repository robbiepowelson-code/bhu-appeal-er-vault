Declaration of Emily Roznowski in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion for Temporary Restraining Order
I, Emily Roznowski, declare as follows:
1. I am an attorney with Disability Rights Advocates, attorney of record for Plaintiffs. I 
have personal knowledge of the facts set forth herein. If called as a witness, I could and would 
competently testify to the matters stated herein. I make this declaration in support of Plaintiffs’ Motion 
for Temporary Restraining Order.
2. During the week of February 16, 2026, the City of Berkeley posted a series of Notices of 
Encampment Closures at the Harrison encampment indicating that residents must vacate by February 24, 
2026. The Notices did not identify a location where encampment residents could safely relocate, the 
Notices stated that the City will not store any property left behind due to the leptospirosis outbreak, and 
the Notices stated that any remaining residents would be subject to arrest.
3. On February 19, 2026, I sent a Demand Letter to the City stating that the City’s planned 
closure would be unlawful and would cause immense harm to Prado Plaintiffs and the Putative Class. In 
the letter, Plaintiffs’ counsel requested that, among other things, the City identify a safe relocation site or 
sites for residents, provide assistance to individuals living in vehicles to relocate with their vehicles to 
the designated safe relocation site, and replace all survival gear of which the City intended to dispose. I
asked the City to respond by 5:00 p.m. on Friday, February 20, 2026. The letter informed the City that if 
they failed to respond, Plaintiffs would seek a Temporary Restraining Order. A true and correct copy of 
this Letter is attached as Exhibit A.
4. On February 21, 2026, counsel for the City sent an email to me stating that it would 
postpone the abatement identified in the February 18 and February 19 notices. The Parties agreed to 
meet and confer over the City’s encampment resolution policies.
5. On February 23, 2026, the Parties met and conferred. The City agreed to postpone the 
planned Harrison encampment abatement through at least February 28, 2026 and to re-notice any future 
abatements.
6. On February 26, 2026, the Parties met and conferred again and counsel for the City 
informed me that the Berkeley City Council would consider Plaintiffs’ demands at a closed session on 
March 2, 2026.

8. On March 3, 2026, the Parties met and conferred and counsel for the City informed me
