drivers come down the street, and if they do move, they become aggressive at the driver by 
yelling threats at them. 
6. I have personally observed individuals yelling, cursing, and threatening
drivers while vehicles attempt to pass. On multiple occasions, individuals have threatened drivers 
with rocks, pallet guns, dogs, or other objects while vehicles were in motion. These conditions 
make it difficult for customers, delivery vehicles, rideshare drivers, tow-truck drivers, and 
employees to safely access our business. I regularly receive customer and employee 
complaints regarding safety and access issues. This year alone, these complaints have 
numbered in the hundreds. Some customers have said they do not feel that it is safe to enter our 
business because of the encampment activity. This year has been the worst year I have ever 
witnessed, and the most aggressive and widespread the encampment has ever been.
