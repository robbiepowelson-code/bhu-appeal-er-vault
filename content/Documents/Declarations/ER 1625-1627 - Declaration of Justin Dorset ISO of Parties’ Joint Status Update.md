I, Justin Dorset, declare as follows: 
1. I have personal knowledge of the facts set forth in this declaration and, if called as
a witness, could and would testify competently to the matters set forth below. 
2. I am the owner of Dorset Finds, a business located at 1201 10th Street, Berkeley,
California. I have been the owner of Dorset Finds for over fifteen years. Dorset Finds has 
operated at its current location for approximately nine and a half years. 
3. My business is located near the Harrison Corridor encampment area. Because of
this proximity, I regularly observe conditions in and around the encampment while operating my 
business.

4. I have observed individuals wandering back and forth near my business, 
sometimes yelling or screaming loudly, including screaming at themselves and at others. These 
conditions occur during normal business hours. I have observed aggressive shouting and 
confrontations near my business, including individuals yelling, cursing, and arguing with one 
another. I have experienced aggressive shouting directed at me, including being shouted at by 
individuals near the encampment while driving or while near my business. 
5. On multiple occasions, customers have complained to me about the conditions 
near the encampment. Over time, I have received over a dozen complaints expressing concern 
regarding the encampment conditions. 
6. Nearby roadways, including portions of Harrison Street, have effectively 
become one-way streets because one side of the roadway is blocked by encampment-related 
items. I have observed people walking, and riding their bikes down the middle of the road, 
swerving back and forth and not paying attention to traffic. I believe these conditions create a risk 
for both pedestrians and drivers. 
7. I have personally observed close calls involving vehicles, bicycles, and pedestrians 
due to limited roadway visibility and blocked lanes. While I am not aware of anyone being 
injured, I have observed situations where accidents appeared possible. Because navigation 
applications sometimes route drivers down Harrison Street when nearby streets are congested, I 
am concerned that drivers unfamiliar with the area may encounter these conditions unexpectedly 
while driving down Harrison street without realizing they are about to approach road blockages 
where people are residing, and walking in the street. I am extremely concerned that these 
conditions will eventually lead to a vehicle crashing into the encampment, or hitting one of the 
campers who may be walking in the road. I am concerned that, given the current conditions along 
the corridor, it is only a matter of time before an accident occurs, particularly involving vehicles, 
bicycles, or pedestrians navigating the area. 
8. Encampments residents often barbecue within the encampment. I am aware 
of multiple instances of fires that have happened in the surrounding area. I have personally 
observed burning odors and smoke coming from the encampment area on multiple occasions.
