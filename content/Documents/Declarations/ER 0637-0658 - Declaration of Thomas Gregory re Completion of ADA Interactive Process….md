I, Thomas Gregory, declare as follows:
1. I have personal knowledge of the facts described herein. If called as a witness, I 
could and would testify competently and truthfully to the facts set forth in this declaration. 
2. I have a Juris Doctor degree from the University of California, Davis—School of 
Law. I am also a Certified ADA Coordinator through the Americans with Disabilities Act 
(“ADA”) Coordinator Training Certification Program (“ACTCP”) at the Great Plains ADA 
Center. This certification verifies that I have completed training in ADA regulations and 
guidelines.
3. I am the City of Berkeley (“City”) ADA Program Coordinator. My duties include 
receiving and responding to requests made by members of the public who require reasonable 
accommodations to access City programs, activities, and services. Any documents or 
correspondence referred to in this declaration are kept by the City in the regular course of 
business, and it is a regular practice to make and retain such records.
4. In my capacity as ADA Program Coordinator, I regularly receive and evaluate 
requests for reasonable accommodation under Title II of the ADA and related state law and am 
authorized by the City of Berkeley to make determinations as to whether requests for 
accommodations are reasonable under the ADA. In doing so, I assess whether requested 
modifications are reasonable or whether they would fundamentally alter a City program, impose 
an undue administrative or fiscal burden, or require the City to create or develop an entirely new 
program or service. This evaluation is conducted consistent with the requirements of Title II and 
its implementing regulations, including 28 C.F.R. § 35.130(b)(7).
5. In evaluating accommodation requests, it is my normal practice to consult with 
relevant City departments and staff in order to understand the operational impact that a requested 
accommodation would have on the City’s programs and personnel. That information is important 
in determining whether a requested accommodation would impose an undue burden or 
fundamentally alter a City program.
6. When I believe a requested accommodation may need to be denied based on undue 
burden or fundamental alteration, it is also common practice for me to consult with the City
