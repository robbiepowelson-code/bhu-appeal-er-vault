DECLARATION OF CHRISTOPHER D. JENSEN IN SUPPORT OF CITY OF BERKELEY’S OPPOSTION AND 
REPLY RE MOTION FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT [3:25-CV-01414-EMC] 
I, CHRISTOPHER D. JENSEN, declare as follows: 
1. I am the Assistant City Attorney for the City of Berkeley (“City”). If called as a 
witness, I could and would testify competently and truthfully to the facts and opinions set forth in 
this declaration. 
2. The City served written discovery requests on BHU on September 12 and 26, 
2025. True and correct copies of the City’s First Set of Interrogatories and First Set of Requests 
for Production are attached as Exhibits A and B. 
3. The City received no responses to the requests prior to the due dates, and BHU’s 
counsel refused to engage in the meet and confer process after the due dates. 
4. The City sent a draft joint letter to BHU counsel Anthony Prince on November 19, 
2025 under the trial judge’s discovery rules. The City did not receive a response from BHU’s 
counsel. 
5. After referral of the case to Judge Beeler for discovery purposes, the City sought to 
comply with the Court’s Standing Order but still received no response from BHU’s counsel. The 
City submitted a discovery letter to the Court on November 26, 2025. [[ER 1645-1660 - Discovery Dispute Letter|ECF No. 178]]. 
6. In response to the City’s November 26 letter, the Court’s ordered the parties to file 
a joint submission. BHU’s counsel refused to cooperate in the preparation of that submission and 
did not comply with the Court’s order. The City filed a second letter brief on December 19 (ECF 
No. 185). The Court then instructed the parties to file separate opposition and reply briefs (see 
ECF Nos. 186). Both parties complied with this order. 
7. On December 28, the Court ordered BHU’s counsel “to provide his proposed 
deadlines in the update about when he can produce the discovery.” ECF No. 191. BHU’s counsel 
did not comply with the Court’s order. 
8. On January 8, 2026, following a discovery hearing, the Court ordered BHU to 
respond to the outstanding discovery requests by January 29 and that all objections were waived. 
ECF No. 96 
9. On January 28, 2026, BHU’s counsel notified the Court via email that he would 
not comply with the Court’s January 8 order. A true and correct copy of the email from BHU
