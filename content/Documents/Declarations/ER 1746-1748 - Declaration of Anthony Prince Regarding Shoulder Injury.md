I, Anthony D. Prince, hereby swear and affirm as follows: 
1. I am attorney of record for Plaintiffs in the above-captioned case.
2. On October 7, 2025 I was seen in the Emergency Room at Alta Bates (Sutter) Hospital
in Berkeley following an accident in which I fractured 
3. my left shoulder. Prior to the injury I had been working on plaintiff’s portion of the
Joint Status report that was due on October 8. I notified city attorney Marc Shapp that 
I would be unable to provide plaintiff’s report due for inclusion in the Report due to 
the accident. Attached hereto as Exhibit “A” is a true and correct copy of the email I 
sent to Mr. Shapp.

determined by ER physician Dr. Starr that I had fractured my left shoulder scapula, the 
largest of the bones that comprise the ball and socket shoulder joint, that bone 
fragments had begun moving to the rotator cuff area and that surgery would almost 
certainly be necessary. I was provided with a restraining sling to immobilize my left 
arm and shoulder, prescribed pain medication and directed to make an appointment 
with orthopedic surgeon Basil Alwattar. Attached hereto as Exhibit “B” is a true and

Alwattar prepared at my request.

appointment with Dr. Alwattar is scheduled for Friday, Oct. 24.

8. Before the injury it was my intention to file a response to the City’s opposition to our 
briefs on the interactive process and the scope of the Court’s June 10 order for a 
limited preliminary injunction. Among other things, the City’s “survey” of persons in 
the 8th and Harrison encampment does not account for the status of persons who were 
ejected from the encampment because of the June 4 raid but who on June 10 had 
applications on file for disability accommodations that had been filed weeks and 
months before. Had they not been ejected on June 4, they would have been physically 
present on June 10. 
9. Currently the Court order requires the interactive process to be completed by October 
31, 2025. Previously, the Court took the scheduled Status Conference off calendar, but 
I understand today’s order to be anticipating a report from plaintiffs for which 
additional time may be provided by the Court upon receipt of the medical 
documentation attached hereto. It is likely that when I am seen by Dr. Alwatter on 
Friday that surgery will be scheduled soon thereafter. I am requesting that the deadline 
for completion of the interactive process be continued to a date to be determined after I 
am sufficiently recovered from whatever surgery is necessary so that with the aid of 
the above-described technological assistance and other help, I can competently 
represent my clients. 
I swear and affirm under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States and the 
State of California that the above is a true statement.
