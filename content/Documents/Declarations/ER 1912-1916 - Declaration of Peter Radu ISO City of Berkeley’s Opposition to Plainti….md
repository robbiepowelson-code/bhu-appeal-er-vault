I, Peter Radu, declare as follows: 
1. I have personal knowledge of the facts described herein. If called as a witness, I could 
and would testify competently and truthfully to the facts set forth in this declaration. 
2. I am an Assistant to the City Manager for the City of Berkeley (City), and have 
been in that role since August 2021. In this capacity, I serve as the lead on projects associated 
with the unsheltered homeless population in Berkeley. I have over thirteen years of experience 
working with the unhoused population. I was the Homeless Policy Director for the City of 
Oakland from 2019-2021, and prior to that I served as the Homeless Services Coordinator for the 
City of Berkeley from 2017 to 2019. 
3. I received my B.A. in psychology from Stanford University in 2009, and my 
Master of Social Welfare and Master of Public Policy from the University of California, Berkeley 
in 2016. 
4. I have read the Plaintiffs' Motion for Temporary Restraining Order, and make this 
declaration in support of the City's opposition thereto. The correspondence and memoranda 
referred to in this declaration are kept by the City in the regular course of business, and it is a 
regular practice to make and retain such records. 
5. On December 6, 2024, the Neighborhood Services Division and Environmental 
Health Division of the City of Berkeley conducted a site visit at the encampments in and along 
Harrison Street, 8th Street, and the surrounding areas in West Berkeley (the "Harrison Corridor"). 
The site visit revealed significant health and safety hazards including loose and scattered 
syringes; rodent harborage conditions and evidence of extensive and active rodent burrows; 
unmitigated human and animal waste; and open/rotting sources of food. The sidewalks and

6. Based on these documented findings of the dangerous nuisance conditions, on

7. The Berkeley Municipal Code requires the City allow for a hearing when a health 
hazard and nuisance is declared. Therefore, notices were posted on January 7, 2025, informing 
residents that the Harrison Corridor encampment would be subject to closure at any time after 
February 10, 2025 in order to allow time for everyone to prepare for the hearing, for the hearing 
officer to issue a ruling, and for people to have a reasonable amount of time after the ruling to 
comply if the declaration was upheld. Posting notices more than one month before the compliance 
deadline was also intended to provide sufficient time for individuals with disability-related needs 
to raise any reasonable accommodation requests to the City. 
8. On January 29, 2025, the City's Administrative Hearing Officer issued a ruling 
overruling the appellants objections and affirming the City's ability to conduct a nuisance 
abatement, and the City posted a second notice on January 31, 2025.

10. Scheduling a large operation like an encampment closure, where many staff and 
equipment are pulled from across multiple departments, necessarily comes with an opportunity 
cost of foregoing other work and performing other City services. Rescheduling an encampment 
closure can have a cascading effect on other city operations. For example, pulling a backhoe from 
a time-bound construction project can lead to delays, change orders, and costly overruns. 
11. In my experience, publicizing the exact date of an encampment closure invites a 
large number of people to protest at the event, which can create safety issues in crowd control and 
management for everyone. Moreover, the nuisance abatement procedures specified in Berkeley 
Municipal Code ("BMC") City "shall have the power to abate such nuisance without further 
notice including the power to condemn and destroy any property constituting the nuisance if the 
nuisance cannot be abated without destruction of such property." BMC § 11.40.110. 1 This section 
of the BMC was provided as advanced notice to the encampment residents in the January 7, 2025 
and January 31, 2025 public notices posted throughout the Harrison Corridor. See Declaration of 
Okeya Vance-Dozier, filed concurrently herewith, Exs. A and C.
