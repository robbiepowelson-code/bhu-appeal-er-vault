a witness, could and would testify competently to the matters set forth below. 
2. I am the Executive Director of Urban Adamah, a nonprofit organization that hosts
retreats where program participants stay on our property, and we have a two-acre farm. I have 
served in the role of Executive Director for approximately six and a half years. 
3. Urban Adamah has operated at its current location for approximately nine years.

4. The Harrison Corridor encampment is located immediately along our frontage on
Harrison Street. Because of this proximity, I regularly observe conditions in and around the 
encampment during the course of our operations. 
5. One of the most significant concerns to me regarding the encampments is the fire
risk associated with the encampments. I have observed open flames on multiple occasions, as 
well as other fires within the encampment, including fires that required response by the Berkeley 
Fire Department. 
6. I have observed tents and structures constructed from highly flammable material
such as wood pallets located adjacent to large residential and commercial buildings. Based on 
their proximity and construction, I am concerned that if a fire were to occur, it could spread 
quickly to neighboring structures, including our facilities. 
7. I have observed large fires, and on multiple occasions I have smelled burning
smoke and plastic-like odors coming from the encampment. I have been concerned for the health 
of my staff and program participants from them breathing in this smoke and the odors that come 
from the encampments. 
8. During a recent fire, Urban Adamah had to evacuate participants that were on our
property participating in one of our programs because we were concerned about smoke exposure 
and safety. At the time, we did not know whether the fire could spread. 
9. The encampment conditions have caused me significant concern for the safety of
our staff, program participants, neighboring residents, and the individuals living in the 
encampments. 
10. Urban Adamah has experienced theft and unauthorized entry onto our property.
On approximately three to five occasions, individuals climbed over our fence or found ways to 
gain entry to the property. These incidents were captured on security video.
