Pursuant to Rules 26 and 33 of the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure, Defendant The City 
of Berkeley (“Defendant”), by and through its attorneys, requests that Plaintiff Berkeley
Homeless Union (“Plaintiff”) responds to the following interrogatories requested below, in 
accordance with the definitions and instructions herein, within thirty (30) days of service of these 
interrogatories.

1. The terms “YOU” and “YOUR” shall refer to Plaintiff, INCLUDING its 
predecessors or successors, parents, subsidiaries, divisions, affiliates, any joint venture to which it 
may be a party, and/or each of its employees, officers, directors, agents, representatives, 
consultants, attorneys, accountants and investigators. 
2. The term “PERSON” is defined as any natural person or any legal entity, 
INCLUDING any business or governmental entity or association. 
3. The term “CONCERNING” means relating to, referring to, describing, evidencing, 
or constituting.
4. The term “MEMBER” shall have the same meaning as relied upon in Plaintiff’s 
Complaint.
5. The term “BERKELEY HOMELESS UNION” or “BHU” shall refer to Plaintiff, 
the unincorporated membership organization defined in Plaintiff’s Complaint.
6. The term “RESIDENT” or “RESIDE” shall refer an individual who stays 
overnight or lodges in a particular location.
