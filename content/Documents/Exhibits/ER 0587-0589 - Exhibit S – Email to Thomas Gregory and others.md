Hi Mr. Gregory,
I am writing to seek urgent clarification regarding the status of ADA accommodation requests submitted on behalf of our
members, particularly concerning the March 10 extension and the City's "final determinations."
Given that enforcement is scheduled to begin in less than 24 hours, we need clear answers to ensure our members
understand their status and can prepare accordingly.
1. Does the March 10 Extension Apply to Everyone?
Your recent responses have been inconsistent. Some individuals received explicit extensions to March 10, while others
did not.
Please confirm in writing by the end of the day, which individuals are covered by the March 10 extension. Our
members need to know whether they will be subject to enforcement at 6:00 a.m. tomorrow or Wednesday.
2. What Does the Extension Actually Mean?
Your responses granting the extension also denied every substantive accommodation request (safe relocation site,
shelter, physical assistance, storage, ADA-exempted footprint, 30-day notice, sanitation services, etc.).
Please clarify:
Does the extension mean the City will pause all enforcement—including displacement, citation, arrest, property
removal, and vehicle towing—until March 10?
Does the extension apply only to the individuals' physical presence, or does it also protect their belongings, tents,
RVs, vans, and support animals from removal or destruction?
If an individual has received a "final determination" denying their accommodations, does that mean the interactive
process is completely over for them, or does the City remain open to further dialogue until March 10?
Who made the "fundamental alteration" or "undue burden" determination, and what evidence supports it?
3. What Is the Status of the Interactive Process?
You have declared "final determinations" for many individuals. However, the ADA requires an ongoing interactive process,
not a one-time denial.
Please clarify for each individual. If the process is not complete, please provide:
Proposed dates for in-person meetings (low-stimulation with peer support)
Proposed alternatives for denied accommodations other than a day extension
A timeline for completing the process before enforcement

Furthermore, for each denial based on "fundamental alteration" or "undue burden," please provide the specific, evidencebased justification required by the ADA, including:
a. What resources were considered?
b. What alternatives were analyzed?
c. Why was no alternative feasible?
d. Did the City take "any other action" to ensure that individuals with disabilities received services "to the
maximum extent possible" after denying their requests, as required by 28 C.F.R. § 35.164? If so, please describe those
actions.
4. What Happens on March 11?
Please clarify the City's operational plans:
Will enforcement begin promptly at 6:00 a.m., or is there a grace period?
Will individuals be given on-site verbal warnings before property is removed or vehicles are towed?
Will City staff be available to answer questions and provide assistance during enforcement?
Will medical personnel be on-site to address medical emergencies (seizures, cardiac events, panic attacks)?
How will the City handle individuals who are asleep, disoriented, or in medical distress?
Please respond as soon as possible. Despite the City posting notices four separate times, no outreach worker has ever
come to explain them. Campers have explicitly asked for clarification, including myself—and received none. The notices
remain unexplained and inaccessible to camp residents.
Thank you for your attention to this urgent matter.
Sincerely,
Yesica Prado
