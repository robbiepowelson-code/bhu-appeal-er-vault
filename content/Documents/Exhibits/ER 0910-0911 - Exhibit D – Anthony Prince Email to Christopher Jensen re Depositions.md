Mr. Jensen:
Last night | was provided with a letter that Peter Radu sent to BHU, but not to me, which, among other things, announces
that the City intends to close the 8th and Harrison encampment "as soon as possible" and demands that requests for
reasonable accommodations be submitted no later than "January 23, 2025."
| assume that that "2025" is a typographical error and that the deadline is actually January 23, 2026, which is two days
from now.
As you know, there are persons in the camp other than those covered by Judge Chen's order who we represent and, in
any case, persons who have a right under the law to seek and received reasonable accommodations regarding their
imminent displacement.
Today is a Voluntary Clean Up day that was announced by the City in which Union President Yesica Prado is
participating. As the representative of the BHU, Ms. Prado has the right to be present at today's deposition. Equally
problematic is the City's unilaterally imposed 48-hour deadline for reasonable accomodations requests.
| previously notified the City that we would be filing an ex parte motion to protect the rights of those covered by Judge
Chen's order as well as others in the encampment in light of the City's determination to close the camp. Mr. Radu's letter
makes it all the more urgent that we move forward to file the motion. | will also be writing to the City Attorney's Office in
response to Mr. Radu's letter.
For now, this is to advise the City that the Union is not agreeable to the timetable proposed by Mr. Radu and objects to
the unilaterally imposed deadline for submission of requests for reasonable accommodations.
For the reasons provide above, | cannot afford to spend either today or tomorrow defending depositions and, unless
otherwise resolved, the depositions will not go forward.
If you wish to discuss this further, please call.
Anthony Prince
