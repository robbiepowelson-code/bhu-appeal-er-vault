On Feb 18, 2026, at 8:15 AM, Gerstein, Beth <BGerstein@berkeleyca.gov> wrote:
Dear Gilman District Stakeholder,
On Tues., Feb. 17, the City Manager's office shared the attached notice of partial closure of the Harrison/Eighth Street
encampments. As you may be aware, the judge in the case has allowed the City of Berkeley to move forward with
closing the encampments for all non-protected plaintiffs in the Berkeley Homeless Union v. City of Berkeley lawsuit,
provided the City gives at least seven days advanced written notice.
The notice of partial closure sets next Tues., Feb. 24 as the compliance deadline. This means the City could enforce the
encampment closure any time on or after Feb. 24.
The purpose of this operation is to clear as much of the encampment as possible in order to enable Alameda County
vector control to assess the area for a comprehensive strategy to initiate rodent abatement - the main strategy to
eliminate the leptospirosis threat from the corridor. Insofar as both tents/structures and also vehicles are potentially
sources of rodent harborage, the City's strategy is to prevent re-encampment/continuous parking for the purposes of
enabling effective, ongoing vector control. As such, the City will:
1. Begin enforcing the existing "no lodging" signage that was posted throughout the corridor last summer (except for
the encampments belonging to six plaintiffs who the federal judge has explicitly ordered the City to keep in place);
and
2. Post 'no parking/tow away' signage for all of Harrison St, from 6th-9th and all of Eighth from Gilman to
Codornices Creek, for the time period 2/24-3/27. THIS WILL APPLY TO ALL VEHICLES PARKED AT ANY
TIME ON THOSE STREETS (except those belonging to two protected plaintiffs, each of whom has an RV
on the east side of Eighth near Harrison, which the judge has explicitly ordered the City to keep in place).
The initial assessment is that vector control efforts will take at least 30 days to effectuate (with actual timelines and
strategies subject to how much of the plaintiffs' debris remains after the partial closure) - hence the no parking/tow away
timeline above: February 24 through March 27.
I am sharing this information now as the upcoming parking restrictions, which start next week, may have an impact on
your business and employees as well as yourself. I invite you to contact my office with questions or concerns.
Thank you,
