## ER-628

Internal
The following sections of the Berkeley Municipal Code (BMC) are referenced in this 
Environmental Health inspection report. These code provisions regulate environmental health 
conditions that may create rodent harborage, unsanitary conditions, vector breeding, 
stormwater contamination, or other public health hazards.
No person shall permit any premises, building, structure, vehicle, or other place to be 
maintained in a condition that allows rodents to harbor, breed, or multiply. 
Conditions that may violate this section include:
Rat burrows observed on or adjacent to property or vehicles
Rodent droppings indicating active infestation
Vehicles or structures providing shelter for rodents
Dense vegetation or debris supporting rodent activity
Premises or vehicles maintained in a condition that allows rodents to inhabit or breed may 
constitute rodent harborage under this section. 
No person shall possess, occupy, or maintain, or cause or permit another person to occupy or 
maintain, any building, structure, vehicle, or any other place in such a condition as will permit 
the breeding or harboring therein, or thereon, of rodents or any other vermin.
Conditions that may violate this section include:
Vehicles with cavities or openings that provide rodent shelter
Open engine compartments or structural openings
Storage conditions allowing rodents to inhabit vehicles or surrounding areas
Debris or materials creating rodent nesting areas
Vehicles observed with open compartments, structural openings, or stored materials capable of 
supporting rodent nesting may constitute rodent harborage under this section.

## ER-629

Internal
No person shall place, leave, dump, or permit to accumulate any garbage or rubbish in or upon 
any building, structure, or place so that the same shall afford food or harborage for rodents. 
No person shall accumulate or permit the accumulation on any place, premises, or open lot of 
any lumber, building material, boxes, paper, rags, excess or dense vegetation, or any other 
material that may serve as rodent harborage unless such materials are stored in a manner that 
prevents rodent habitation. 
Conditions addressed by this section include: 
Accumulation of debris or discarded materials
Storage of furniture, pallets, or building materials outdoors
Storage of personal property that creates rodent nesting areas
Dense vegetation combined with debris accumulation
Vehicles surrounded by stored materials, debris piles, or vegetation capable of supporting rodent 
activity may violate this section. 
This section authorizes Environmental Health officials to identify and abate conditions that 
constitute imminent health hazards when such conditions present a threat to public health or 
safety. 
Conditions that may be considered imminent health hazards include: 
Biohazardous waste such as hypodermic needles
Environmental Health inspectors may determine that certain conditions represent 
imminent hazards requiring corrective action or abatement under this section. 
It is unlawful for any person to place or cause to be placed anywhere upon any sidewalk, 
parklet, or roadway any object that obstructs, restricts, or prevents the use of any portion 
of such sidewalk, parklet, or roadway except as authorized by the City.
