Good day yesterday working together with the team from UC Davis who were taking blood and
urine samples from the dogs at the 8th and Harrison encampment as part of a wider study of
leptospirosis and how it impacts the dog population.
Every dog at this encampment and many far beyond this camp has been vaccinated by Paw Fund
as part of our response to the two cases found in dogs from that area.
So yesterday’s effort wasn’t about finding sick infected dogs - there have been no new cases since
the two last December - it was about testing all these pets as part of a wider study which might be
used in future research to develop better prevention.
We’d like to thank Dr Jane Sykes and her team for including us in this project because they
understand that we have a commitment to animal health through community outreach and we want
to especially thank all the pet owners who brought their dogs, held them tight during what was an
invasive procedure and trusted in this process.
Partnerships - they work.
