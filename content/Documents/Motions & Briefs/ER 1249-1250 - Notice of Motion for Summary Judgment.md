NOTICE OF MOTION FOR SUMMARY JUDGMENT 
TO ALL PARTIES AND THEIR COUNSEL OF RECORD:
PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that on March 20, 2026, at 10:00 a.m., in Courtroom #5, 17th 
Floor, or at any other time as the Court may order, Defendants City of Berkeley (“City”), Paul 
Buddenhagen, Thomas Gregory, Peter Radu, and Okeya Vance-Dozier (collectively, 
“Defendants”) will move this Court, pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 56(a) for 
summary judgment in their favor against Plaintiff BERKELEY HOMELESS UNION (“BHU”). 
Defendants make their motion on the grounds that (1) BHU has not offered concrete 
evidence to support its standing allegations; (2) there is no dispute of material fact that the City 
complied with the ADA; (3) BHU has not and cannot support its claim that the City subjects its 
members to state created danger; and (4) BHU has not established that the City has seized its 
property in violation of the Fourth Amendment. 
This motion is based upon this Notice of Motion, the Memorandum of Points and 
Authorities, the accompanying declarations of Marc Shapp, David White, Peter Radu, Okeya 
Vance-Dozier, Thomas Gregory, and Ron Restani; the pleadings and papers on file; and any oral 
argument that may be permitted by the Court.
