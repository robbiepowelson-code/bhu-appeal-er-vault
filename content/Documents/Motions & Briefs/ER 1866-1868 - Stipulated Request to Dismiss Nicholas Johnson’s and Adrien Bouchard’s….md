STIPULATED REQUEST TO DISMISS NICHOLAS JOHNSON’S AND ADRIEN BOUCHARD’S CLAIMS 
PURSUANT TO FEDERAL RULE OF CIVIL PROCEDURE 41 (a)(2) and PROPOSED ORDER
Pursuant to Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 41, Plaintiffs Adrien Bouchard and Nicholas 
Johnson and all Defendants, by and through their counsel of record, hereby respectfully request that the 
Court dismiss Nicholas Johnson’s claims and Adrien Bouchard’s claims from this matter with prejudice 
with the Court to retain jurisdiction to enforce the terms of the settlement as set forth in paragraph 7.1 of 
the Settlement Agreement among the parties hereto and attached as Exhibit 1. 
This stipulation and request is not intended to dismiss or otherwise change Plaintiffs Berkeley 
Homeless Union’s or Frank Moore’s claims in this matter.
